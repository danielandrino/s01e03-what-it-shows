How to tell them apart

Read the report’s tenant, not its intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser; the cause depends on where the tenant is and which booking form it runs.

  • Hint. The report came through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people typed what it said, and the form read it day first. The parser fix did not touch it.
  • Parser. Every other report from a day-first tenant. The shared parser read month first, so any date with a day of 12 or less came out swapped.

Some parser reports describe a date that moved by one day, not a swap. The desk lists them in desk-notes.json; they are filed under the parser by tenant, and the table footnotes them.

Lessons

  • A merge on subject counts symptoms, not causes. Before marking a big ticket known, group it by tenant and channel.
  • The intake tag disagrees with the cause often enough that it cannot be used to count.
