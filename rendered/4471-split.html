How to tell them apart

Read the report’s tenant, not its intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser; the cause depends on where the tenant is and which booking form it runs.

  1. One-day move. The date moves by exactly one day, or two viewers see two days. A day/month swap cannot do that: 12 May swapped is 5 December, not 11 May. Looks like a time zone shift on all-day dates. The desk already lists these in desk-notes.json; until now they were counted under the parser.
  2. Hint. The report came through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type what it says, and the form reads it day first.
  3. Parser. Everything else. The shared parser reads month first and every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out swapped.

Lessons
